    What the simulator does and doesn't tell you
    • Each virtual source is synthesised, delayed to every mic by distance ÷ speed of sound, and mixed with first- and second-order wall reflections, a diffuse echo tail and independent mic noise. The locator only ever sees the four mic signals.
    • The simulator's geometry matches the locator's exactly, so errors near 0 cm here are a best case. A real room adds mic-position error, furniture, directional talkers and longer reverb. Expect roughly 10–30 cm.
    • Try "Hum": a steady tone repeats every cycle, so its arrival-time difference is ambiguous and it can't be located. Claps, speech and broadband noise can.
    • Move two talkers together and watch them merge at around half a metre apart.
