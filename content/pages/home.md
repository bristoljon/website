I build web and mobile apps. Before that I patched people up in the back of an ambulance.

I spent five years as a paramedic in central London, first on an ambulance crew and later as a solo responder. After a stint as a volunteer expedition medic in Costa Rica, I taught myself web development from scratch. This site was the thing I learnt on.

Since then I've built React and React Native apps for property data, commodity prices, home automation and 3D city planning. I've led teams and set up engineering from the ground up. These days I'm Industry Platform Lead at Ecotricity, where I recently built an AI assistant for the contact centre.

Away from the day job I tinker with Arduino gadgets, colour-changing clocks and ideas that are still on the back of an envelope. I also like reading, frisbee and flying kites.

This site is where the side projects live, old and new. Have a poke around, read [the CV](/cv) if you're hiring, and get in touch if something sparks an idea.
